The Starry Night
Post-Impressionism

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year: 1889

Description

"The Starry Night" depicts the view from the east-facing window of Van Gogh's asylum room at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, featuring sweeping brushstrokes, swirling skies, and vibrant blues and yellows. It stands as one of the most famous pieces in modern art history.

Mona Lisa
Italian Renaissance

Artist: Leonardo da Vinci

Year: 1503

Description

The Mona Lisa is a half-length portrait painting considered an archetypal masterpiece of the Italian Renaissance. It is famed for its subject's enigmatic expression, monumental composition, subtle modeling of forms, and atmospheric illusionism.

Girl with a Pearl Earring
Dutch Golden Age

Artist: Johannes Vermeer

Year: 1665

Description

Known as the "Mona Lisa of the North," this oil painting depicts a European girl wearing an exotic dress, an oriental turban, and a large pearl earring. The subtle use of light highlighting her eyes and lips showcases Vermeer's technical mastery.

The Scream
Expressionism

Artist: Edvard Munch

Year: 1893

Description

"The Scream" is the popular name given to a composition created by Norwegian artist Edvard Munch. The agonizing face has become one of the most iconic images of art, symbolizing the human anxiety and existential dread of the modern human condition.

Water Lilies
Impressionism

Artist: Claude Monet

Year: 1919

Description

Water Lilies is a series of approximately 250 oil paintings by French Impressionist Claude Monet. The paintings depict his flower garden at his home in Giverny and were the main focus of his artistic production during the last thirty years of his life.
